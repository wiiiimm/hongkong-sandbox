Nicholas Tse Appointed as Celebrity Ambassador for Upton
2014 / 12 / 03

Upton, the luxury residential project by Emperor International Holdings Limited, featuring stunning vistas of Victoria Harbour, is ready to be launched. Situated at the water’s edge, Upton offers uninterrupted sea views from every unit. The developer today announced the appointment of Mr. Nicholas Tse as celebrity ambassador to showcase the distinctive lifestyle of the project and unveiled the floor plan of a typical four-bedroom unit with a storeroom.

 

Mr. Donald Cheung, the Executive Director of Emperor International, said, “Strategically located close to Victoria Harbour, Upton boasts sweeping views of the world-renowned Victoria Harbour skyline from every unit. The tower’s façade features a unique wave pattern which reflects the fluidity of water while the architectural beauty of the project is fully enhanced in the splendid metropolitan environment. Today we are pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Nicholas Tse as the ambassador of the project. This special occasion also serves as the kick-off of our upcoming promotional programmes.”

 

Mr. Cheung added, “Mr. Nicholas Tse, as a famous and elite celebrity in Hong Kong, is well known for his unique and noble lifestyle. His recent TV programme has been very popular and showcased his discerning taste for high-end style and luxury. This perfectly matches the project’s name Upton as well as the project concept and positioning. Thus we are very glad to have Mr. Tse to be Upton’s ambassador to interpret the first-class, extraordinary experience that can be enjoyed in Upton. Mr. Tse also stars in our Upton TV commercial which will soon be launched across various media platforms.”

 

Emperor International today also presented the floor plan of a typical four-bedroomed unit with a storeroom. There are totally 24 units of this layout, distributed from 25th to 33th floor, and from 35th to 37th floor. With only 3 flats on each floor, residents are assured of a high degree of privacy. Unit 37A features a saleable area of 1,753 square feet and a floor-to-floor height of 3.5 meters. A primary-secondary door design for the entrance adds a sense of prestige and glamour. The spacious living and dining area spans over 400 square feet and are clearly defined. Glass curtain wall and hinge doors in front of the terrace afford ample amount of natural light for a beautifully bright home.

 

There are two master bedrooms with a saleable area of over 300 square feet each in this unit. A cloakroom is also available for residents to enjoy the high quality of life in Upton. Besides, the bathtubs in both bathrooms inside the master bedrooms are designed with a massage function. Together with the expansive windows at the side of the bathtubs which provide panoramic views of Victoria Harbour, bathing moments in Upton are so unique and special than ever before.
