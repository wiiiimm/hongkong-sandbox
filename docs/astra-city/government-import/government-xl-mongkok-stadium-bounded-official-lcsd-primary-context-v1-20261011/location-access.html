Location & Access

 

Location Map

 

Public Transport

The Mong Kok Stadium is conveniently accessible by MTR or other forms of public transportation including buses and minibuses.

 

MTR

  1. Prince Edward Station → Around 10-minute walk along Playing Field Road from Exit A of Prince Edward Station towards the direction of Flower Market Road.
  2. Mong Kok East Station → Around 10-minute walk through MOKO from Exit D of Mong Kok East Station towards the direction of Royal Plaza Hotel, via the footbridge to Flower Market Road.
