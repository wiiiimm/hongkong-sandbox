## Contract and results

requires exact source/identity/current hashes, original mesh bytes, no existing
holds or registry landmark scope, tighter footprint fit, mobile runtime budgets
and conservative contact against **actual rendered terrain triangles**. It checks
all source vertices and triangle centres plus lower edges at <=1 m spacing.

0.1 m above ground and limits lower-rim gaps to 1 m. Sampler/drawn terrain agreement
must be within 0.004 m. This is a faithful source-port contract; it does not certify
whole architecture, survey-grade foundations or exhaustive triangle intersections.
