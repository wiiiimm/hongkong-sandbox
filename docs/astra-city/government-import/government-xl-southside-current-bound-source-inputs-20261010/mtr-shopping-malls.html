Luxury Mall

ELEMENTS

  • ELEMENTS offers more than one million square feet of pure shopping bliss subtly themed after the five Chinese elements.
  • The mall brings together some of the world's most sought-after brands and services, many of which are either new to Hong Kong or have their flagship stores here. Dining is also a major attraction at ELEMENTS, with big names in both Oriental and Western cuisine. The mall's wraparound glass wall creates have the perfect harbour-view dining experience.
  • The “Artist Square Bridge” located on 2/F Metal Zone at ELEMENTS enables easy access to the West Kowloon Cultural District. Visitors can immerse themselves in the M+ museum of visual culture, the West Kowloon Art Park and Waterfront Promenade.
  • Shopping, leisure, dining, entertainment and culture have never before come together in such a perfect way. The fundamentals of shopping in Hong Kong are evolving, and ELEMENTS is where it's happening.

    • http://www.elementshk.com

Regional Malls

Telford Plaza

  • Located in the buzzing heart of Kowloon East, Telford Plaza is a true retail landmark. A stone’s throw from the Kowloon Bay MTR station, the vibrant mall spans more than 930,000 square feet, and is home to more than 270 international brands.
  • Showcasing the largest variety of retailers in Kowloon East, Telford Plaza is a thriving destination for dining, shopping, leisure and entertainment, and is a second home to the area’s professionals and residents.
  • Telford Plaza offers more than 50 dining options, and a dazzling selection of leading international and local brands on latest fashion, on-trend beauty and cosmetics, designer lifestyle products, electronics or more. Telford Plaza also boasts the only MX4D cinema in the area, a wildly popular Japanese games arcade and an exciting SPORTAINMENT ARENA-making it the perfect location for sharing special occasions and dining, shopping and leisure experiences with friends and family.

    • http://www.telford-plaza.com

Maritime Square

  • Maritime Square is a major connection between various tourism highlights in Hong Kong, bringing delightful sensation to a relaxing weekend.
  • Displaying a flair for trends and dynamics, Maritime Square has numerous modish fashion, beauty, electronics, sports and household retailers, and an unrivaled variety of restaurants parading the 630,000 square feet shopping centre. With over 190 shops and restaurants, Maritime Square serves all you need by offering a dynamic and energetic one-stop shopping experience.
  • Shopping experience is further enriched by the 50,000 square feet green balcony and roof garden at Maritime Square 2, where shoppers can have a taste of relaxed ambience while securing their favorites in Maritime Square.

    • http://www.maritimesquare.com

PopCorn

  • Located in the heart of Tseung Kwan O, PopCorn is a modish shopping destination. This trendy hotspot atop Tseung Kwan O MTR station spans more than 345,000 square feet and equipped with a sophisticated transport network.
  • Brings together over 120 quality stores, offering a wide array of world-renowned cosmetics, fashion and sports brands, coupled with a delightful range of gourmet restaurants.
  • Entertainment is also the core part of PopCorn. This lifestyle hub provides shoppers unique experience with STAR Cinema and Namco - the famous Japanese game centre.

    • http://www.popcorntko.com.hk

The LOHAS

  • The LOHAS is situated at the centre of LOHAS Park, covers a total area encompassing over 480,000 square feet. The LOHAS adopts “Shopping in Nature’s Breeze” as a design concept. The design of The LOHAS integrates the exterior and interior environments infusing different natural elements. Glass curtain walls at both ends of the mall bring outdoor greenery into the mall, while the streamlined corridor channel matches the lighting design of the venue and the atrium. With strong natural lighting, the mall will deliver for customers a fresh and pleasing arrangement, allowing them to enjoy a natural, comfortable atmosphere whilst shopping and enjoying leisure time.
  • The LOHAS brings together nearly 150 merchants; providing entertainment, leisure and community services. The world-class entertainment and leisure facilities include Hong Kong’s largest and first environmentally-friendly ice rink applying the latest energy saving technology which is designed according to international standards, the largest cinema in Tseung Kwan O with six houses, and 366 indoor parking spaces, including 10 electric vehicle charging stations and 33 motorcycle parking spaces. These facilities will offer the 68,000 residents from the 25,500 units of LOHAS Park with new shopping, dining and leisure experiences to suit their daily needs.

    • https://www.thelohasmall.com/en

The Wai

  • A brand new shopping mall atop MTR Tai Wai Station, The Wai combines a central location with the unique advantages of an excellent transport network.
  • Being the only mega mall in the district, it covers a total area of nearly 650,000 square feet and bring nearly 150 merchants. It is set to become a new landmark and bring more exciting experiences to the local residents.
  • The Wai features over 50,000 square feet of green space, creating a vibrant environment for recreation and leisure. It’s also home to the largest indoor bicycle parking lot in Hong Kong, with 330 bicycle parking spaces.

    • https://www.thewaimall.com/en

THE SOUTHSIDE

  • THE SOUTHSIDE, as the landmark shopping mall in Island South, spans around 510,000 square feet. Inspired by the natural beauty and sweeping coastline of Island South, the five-retail floor mall provides a one-stop premium lifestyle experience.
  • THE SOUTHSIDE is home to approximately 130 shops, restaurants, and a cinema. These include various iconic speciality stores and popular gastronomic places, of which some are coming to Island South for the first time.
  • THE SOUTHSIDE dedicates to strive environmental protection by providing more than 230 parking spaces, all of which are equipped with EV charging capabilities. Seamlessly connected to MTR Wong Chuk Hang station, THE SOUTHSIDE is only an 8-minute ride to MTR Admiralty station.
  • THE SOUTHSIDE strives to provide a diverse range of artistic and cultural events and strengthen connections in the local community to bring new vibrancy to Island South.

    • https://www.thesouthside.com.hk/en
