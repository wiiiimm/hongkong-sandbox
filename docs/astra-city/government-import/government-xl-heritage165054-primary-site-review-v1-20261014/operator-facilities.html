Hong Kong Heritage Museum - Facilities

Exhibition Galleries

There are six permanent exhibition galleries for the display of the museum's collections and six thematic galleries for temporary exhibitions.

Theatre

With a seating capacity of 350, the multipurpose theatre is a sophisticated venue for Cantonese opera, musical performances, lectures, seminars, ceremonies and multimedia presentations.

Education Studios and Seminar Rooms

The Seminar Room and the Education Studio are equipped with advanced audio visual equipment. These serve as the primary venues for the museum’s in-house educational and extension activities, such as talks, demonstrations, workshops, and training classes. 

Car Park

Notice of Closure of Hong Kong Heritage Museum Public Car Park
The public car park at the Hong Kong Heritage Museum has been closed from 1 August 2025 onward to facilitate the Trunk Road T4 construction project, and the site has been handed over to the Civil Engineering and Development Department for related works.
We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.  

Baby Care Room Facilities

Our baby care room equipped with changing and feeding facilities is located at the Ground Floor.

Baby Strollers, Wheelchairs and Walking Stick Chairs Lending Service

We also provide baby strollers, wheelchairs and walking stick chairs lending service. Please contact our enquiry counter for further assistance.

Free Bicycle Parking Area

For visitors who cycle to the museum, we have also set up a free bicycle parking area. Please contact our enquiry counter for the location of parking area. However, you should bring your own bicycle lock.

Free Lockers and Luggage Deposit

Larger items must be deposit in reception, free lockers are available for smaller items.

 
The Museum strives to provide a barrier-free access for all. Wheelchairs can be borrowed at the Information Counter at G/F subject to availability. Coaches from rehabilitation centres may drop off visitors at the museum's covered car park which leads directly to the museum lobby.

Currently, barrier-free facilities and services provided at Hong Kong Heritage Museum are:

  • Barrier-free ramp

  • Tactile guide path and Braille and tactile floor plan at the G/F, 1/F and 2/F

  • Automatic door at the foyer

  • Enquiries counter suitable for wheelchair users

  • Assistive listening system at the box office and enquiries counter

  • Accessible toilets

  • Braille and tactile markings and audible signal in passenger lift

  • Audio and visual signals of the emergency alarm system

  • Designated spaces convenient for wheelchair users in Theatre

  • All floors are accessible via lift


Access Officer

The Access Officer of the Hong Kong Heritage Museum is Miss Joey CHO, Manager (Heritage Museum), who can be contacted at 2180 8213 during office hours (9am to 1pm & 2pm to 6pm on Mon to Fri). During non-office hours, please call our Duty Manager at 6706 5430.
