## Decision for a possible later triangle-shaped collar

If a whole retained literal triangle actually intersects an owned projection,
strictly disjoint outward protection enclosing that whole triangle is
impossible at that pair, regardless of collar shape or height separation. This
is infeasibility of the **disjoint protection construction family**, not model
corruption or proof that no other unchanged-source terrain method can work.
Height evidence may show that the literal surface already lies safely below
the original model; it still supplies no foundation, support, coverage or
permission to overlap an additional native layer.

If all actual triangle projections are strictly disjoint and failures are
collar-only, a later separately reviewed candidate could try the convex hull
of each exact retained triangle plus the four signed 1/1024 m XY offsets
(triangle Minkowski-summed with a dyadic square), rather than its enclosing
rectangle. This is a finite triangle-shaped outward construction region, not
an epsilon predicate. Its complete vertices and exact separation against
every owned facet/all four worlds must be proved before any clipping. An
offset that actually contacts source remains a hold. This draft does not
generate those polygons or certify their feasibility.

Neither shape nor height evidence proves genuine owned ground is covered.
Any future proposal must keep every old triplet/grid/sampler ordering literal,
check all newly packed facets, independently prove whole owned finite coverage
and clearance, validate seams/current native/basic actors, and pass the real
existing terrain/runtime validators. Any gap remains a gap. No clipped source,
fabricated height, tolerance widening, shortened retention or automatic
acceptance follows from this diagnostic.
