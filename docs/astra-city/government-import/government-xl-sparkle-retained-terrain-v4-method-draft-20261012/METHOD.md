## Conditions that remain unresolved

This method may fail because a complete retained-face collar genuinely
intersects an owned world. It may also leave genuine terrain coverage holes.
Neither outcome licenses reducing old retention, omitting owned facets,
widening a distance band or inserting hypothetical terrain heights.

A successful constructor would still be only a private, explicitly altered
terrain proposal. Complete owned-source/literal/two-F32 finite coverage and
clearance, strict seams, current 6,649-actor altered-domain checks, raw basic
and native regressions, foundation/support roles, runtime and browser evidence
remain mandatory before any acceptance. Copied scalar fallback and the
existing validator's numerical gap threshold supply no exact seam or owned
coverage credit. No new building, support, structural bridge, native
reapproval or installation credit is supplied by this method.
