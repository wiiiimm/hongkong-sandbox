Kai Tak Cruise Terminal Building

Transcript

Description

The Kai Tak Cruise Terminal Building is iconic and highly functional, together with its sustainable features, it can facilitate the operator to provide world-class services to cruise passengers. It is not only for the cruise passengers, but for the city, for the residents, for the visitors. It's about leisure, cultural occasions, wonderful park along the length of this great building. It's for everybody.
