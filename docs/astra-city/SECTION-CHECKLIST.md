## How to use this list

The 18 administrative districts provide the coverage spine. The smaller sections below are proposed modelling and review areas, not official neighbourhood boundaries. Each includes its surrounding streets, estates, villages, hills, coastline and small islets. The optional **#132** review layer now draws every numbered section. Version `had-2016-project-2026-09-07-v1` partitions the retained official HAD district dataset, including district waters, using existing place anchors and explicit island ownership. Internal borders are approximate review divisions. The source records expose a 2016 lifespan; the retrieval date is not a claimed survey revision. Four saved-place/district conflicts and the airport reclamation split are documented in [the geography audit](review-sections/geography.md).

The readiness ledger currently records **0 Ready, 0 Close to ready, 6 Under review and 126 Base mapped**. Close to ready requires four of the six section-wide checks, including ground and buildings, with evidence and no blockers; Ready requires all six. Local route acceptance or a source import alone cannot promote the wider section. [Overlay use, verification and update workflow](review-sections/README.md).

Use a stable reference such as **01.1 Central core** when choosing the next section. An empty checkbox means its detailed geography and gameplay review remains outstanding; importing geometry alone does not complete it. Split a section further if it is too large for one review.

**Current coverage (fd64435, preserving ff68cf1 source coverage):** 346,115 building forms across 452 tiles, including all 342,223 verified government source records and retained OSM supplements; 196 destination shortcuts. Mui Wo includes 1,327 detailed government models across seven sheets and a 5 m terrain patch; HKS-167 is In Review. Remaining terrain conflicts and estimated geometry are documented in [the extension evidence](mui-wo-buildings/extension/README.md). Most remaining terrain uses the 70 m mesh. Full source coverage and clear arrival points do not complete the detailed section reviews below. See [execution subissues](EXECUTION-ISSUES.md).

**Status key:** Imported base = regional source geometry is available, with completeness and accuracy still unreviewed. Partial base = the current rectangles cut across the area. Mostly pending = only incidental overlap may exist. Pending = no deliberate regional building import yet.

**User priority update:** Lantau, Mui Wo, Tai O, Cheung Chau, Peng Chau and other Lantau villages are the immediate local-review priority. The current build adds village shortcuts and checks their public-path arrivals; detailed modelling, coastline and full walking-route review remains unfinished.

**Earlier suggested review order:** 01.1 Central core → 01.2 waterfront / Admiralty → 01.3 SoHo → 01.4 Sheung Wan → 02.1–02.2 Wan Chai → 05.1–05.2 Tsim Sha Tsui. Then extend across the island and Kowloon, review Lantau / airport, and connect through the New Territories. The numbered districts are coverage references, not a requirement to finish an entire district before visiting another.

## What each section must pass

1. Ground and water: coastline, reclamation date, terrain scale, slope and building-ground alignment.
2. Buildings: footprint coverage, heights with source confidence, recognisable landmarks and skyline.
3. Streets and public space: routes, bridges, stairs, piers, parks and sensible walking access.
4. Exploration: character movement, collision, camera clearance and flight from adjacent sections.
5. Presentation: representative ground and aerial views, day / night, mobile readability and performance.
6. Record source provenance, known approximations and screenshots before marking the section reviewed.
