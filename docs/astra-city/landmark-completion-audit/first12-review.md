# First twelve existing native parts

This pass adds no new models. It reconciles existing government geometry for One/Two IFC, Bank of China Tower, HSBC, Central Plaza, The Center and Cheung Kong Centre against current terrain, support context and normal browser views.

All twelve have exact local asset hashes, actual source-centre terrain/sampler agreement and direct roof picking. Forty accepted desktop/mobile day/night views are indexed in `first12-browser/accepted.json`. Earlier raw reports are retained as capture attempts; only the accepted index selects the final image hashes. Tiny Center annexes were initially hidden by neighbouring buildings and are now captured using actual source-triangle visibility rays. No surrounding city geometry was hidden.

The initial outcome was ten existing parts verified, One IFC tower awaiting required native podium retention metadata, and Center98921 held for a visible night-time speckled façade. Its follow-up is documented separately in `window-seed/decision.json`; this preserves the IFC sidecar's original approval hash. All assets and elevations remain unchanged at1x.

One IFC has73 direct podium contacts among90 low-rim points. The other17 points belong to source triangle components connected to those contacts, with small perimeter overhangs up to2.676m. The required dependency is native podium232441. `dependency-replacements-ifc1.json` changes only tower161250's support metadata, guarded by current catalogue/model hashes. The architect describes the towers and podium as one development with a sculptural IFC2 crown. [Pelli Clarke & Partners](https://pcparch.com/work/ifc-hong-kong)

Triangle counts alone exaggerated foundation intersections because of fine source tessellation. IFC2 tower's hidden faces total0.323m² (0.000326% of its surface); HSBC24.021m² (0.021236%); The Center31.460m² (0.039722%); IFC2 podium41.784m² (0.059442%). The complete native TIN still places The Center's small lowest faces below grade, so lowering the real terrain would be incorrect. HSBC's native TIN comparison retains1206 uncovered sample vertices rather than extrapolating them. These are explicit foundation/plaza limitations, not whole-roof concealment. HSBC's elevated structural frame and open ground-level supports remain visible, consistent with its description of the public plaza. [HSBC](https://www.about.hsbc.com.hk/news-and-media/the-fourth-generation-hsbc-main-building-marks-40th-anniversary)

This acceptance covers native component shape/placement and the recorded foundation context. It does not certify every plaza step, authentic façade materials, interiors, the eight overlapping OSM Center glass components, or complete named-landmark membership. Emulated mobile captures are not physical-device thermal or Safari certification.
