# Buildings coverage — where the city stands, area by area

Companion to [hk-buildings-notes.md](hk-buildings-notes.md). Tracked in Linear under the
milestone **Fable - 3D city — LandsD buildings, night rhythm & sky dial**: parent tracker
HKS-154 with one sub-issue per region — HKS-156 HK Island north · HKS-157 HK Island east &
south · HKS-158 Kowloon · HKS-159 NT west · HKS-160 NT north · HKS-161 NT east · HKS-162
Lantau & airport · HKS-163 outlying islands. Footbridges and elevated walkways: HKS-155.

## The short version

**Data ingest is complete for the whole territory.** The Lands Department Building layer is
territory-wide and the pipeline applies no spatial filter: all 342 224 blocks are in
`3d-viewer/data/hk-buildings.bin`, from Central to Sha Tau Kok, Lantau and every outlying
island. Nothing is imported "region by region".

What *does* vary by area is quality, and that is what the tracker follows:

1. **Terrain fit** — only the Victoria Harbour window (E 826–848 km, N 806–828 km) has the
   20 m mesh; elsewhere the 70 m-averaged territory DEM can sit above true base levels on
   steep slopes and bury lower floors. The *On 20 m mesh* column below says how much of each
   district stands on the fine mesh.
2. **Surveyed height** — blocks without a LandsD height (mostly sheds and canopies) get a
   default and are draped on the ground. Rural districts have many of these.
3. **Verification** — what has actually been looked at in the viewer, by day and by night,
   walked through or flown over. See *Status by region*.

**Rule of thumb from the numbers**: every district above 90 % surveyed height and 100 % on
the fine mesh (the whole harbour core) renders essentially right; the work that remains is in
the New Territories and on the islands, and it is a terrain problem (extend the 20 m mesh,
see the milestone) more than a data problem.

## Regenerating

```
node source-scripts/hk-buildings/report_coverage.mjs \
  --json source-scripts/hk-buildings/coverage.json --md /tmp/coverage.md
```

The first run fetches the 19 boundary polygons from Nominatim (1 request/s) into
`source-scripts/hk-buildings/cache/districts.geojson` (git-ignored); later runs take ~1 s.
