      Abstract: 
          The 3D Pedestrian Network, a vector type dataset, is derived from various source of data and data collected by 
          field checking. It is graphical with attribute data that can be accessed, queried and displayed. It covers 
          the built-up areas of the whole territory, outlying island include Lantau Island, Peng Chau, Cheung Chau, 
          Lamma Island and Ma Wan. It also covers the major footways inside villages, major footpaths inside country 
          parks and the opening hours of the footway inside buildings.
